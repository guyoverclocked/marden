## Name

## Subtitle

A home for ideas worth keeping.

## Short description

Save AI answers, brainstorms, and Markdown in a calm, private library—with optional private sync.

## Full description

The best answer from an AI app should not disappear into chat history. The product idea you shaped during a brainstorm should not be stranded on a clipboard. Marden gives useful text a calm, private home—and gives Markdown the reading experience it deserves.

Paste a Markdown response from your favourite AI app, import `.md` and `.markdown` files from your device, or write from scratch. Organize documents into colour-coded projects or leave them Unfiled, then return to recent reading at any time.

The reader is designed for long-form comfort, with adjustable type, focus mode, persistent reading progress, document outlines, light and dark themes, readable swipeable tables, code blocks, and supported Mermaid flowcharts.

Marden is local-first. No account is required, there are no ads or analytics SDKs, and your library stays on your device. Sign in with Google only when you want to privately sync your library across your own devices.
